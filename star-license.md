We can create original constellation drawings, with Star Almanack holding the copyright, and then deliberately release them under an open license. That preserves authorship while giving other educators, astronomers, programmers, and publishers explicit permission to reuse them.

A permissive choice would be CC BY 4.0, requiring attribution but allowing copying, modification, and commercial reuse. CC BY-SA 4.0 would additionally require adaptations to remain under the same open license.

We should also distinguish the licenses: the artwork and prose can use a Creative Commons license, while any software associated with Star Almanack should use a software license rather than Creative Commons.
